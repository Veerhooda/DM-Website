How we work

We embed for 8–12 weeks. No theater. Every week you see working software, not decks. Decisions are made in Figma and in code, side by side.

We cap at 3 concurrent clients. That’s why we can promise senior attention every day — and why we say no more than yes.

  • Strategy → PrototypeWeek 1–2
  • Build → TestWeek 3–8
  • Launch → MeasureWeek 9–12

What we don’t do

We don’t do “brand exercises” that never touch the product. We don’t staff juniors. We don’t chase trends — we chase clarity.
