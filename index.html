Our Story — Since 2021

Crafted in Jaipur,
for homes everywhere.

We’re Priya & Aman — partners in life and in craft. What began at our kitchen table in Bapu Nagar with a single block-print table and a borrowed kiln is now a sunlit workshop two lanes from home, humming with artisans from Sanganer, Bagru and Molela.

We don’t chase trends. We hunt for honest materials — handloom cotton, terracotta from Molela, rattan and neem wood — and work with families who’ve held these skills for generations. Every piece is touched by hand three to four times: carved, printed, stitched, glazed or woven, then checked by us before it leaves Jaipur.

Why home decor felt broken: fast furniture that looks warm online and tired in a week. We wanted the opposite — pieces that age warm, that look better after a year of sun and touch than on day one. So we sell fewer things, made slower. Home, made warmer isn’t a tagline; it’s our quality test.

Sanganer & Bagru — Block Print
Hand-carved teak blocks, natural dyes, 4-stage wash. Each cushion takes ~6 hours from print to stitch.
◐
Molela — Terracotta
Wheel-thrown, sun-dried 48h, wood-fired. Matte glaze, drainage by hand. No slip casting.
⬡
Barmer — Rattan & Neem
Split rattan woven over neem or bamboo frames, hand-knotted cotton for macramé. Built to last a decade.
